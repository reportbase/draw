tvf the levels tvf.levels.js

The circle in the middle holds everything up to the corner, v = h, in proportion. Past it each ring is one doubling, laid in half the room of the last, crowding toward a horizon it never reaches; a ring narrower than one grain cannot be seen. Lay a shape over it and its outline crosses the levels: the corner is the baseline, and what stands off it, counted in levels, is the shape's depth.

After Serial, Parallel and Nowhere §1, "The corner is the circle, and depth is measured from it" (the reader-geometry paper in the wander repository; the same page is wander's levels.html). Read a shape from its centre or standing on its outline, set your level of detail, and divide out the size. Any shape from the editor opens here ("read the selection in the levels"), and a .tvf file loads from the bar. Every number is measured in your tab.

the circle and its levels a shape across the levels
tvf.levels.js is the levels' arithmetic: the in-place lay (proportion to the corner, then each doubling in half the room of the last, as on the corner), the rings a grain can see, a closed curve read from a standpoint (its centroid, or a place on its outline: the nearest wall in each direction), its broad form, and its depth in levels once its size is divided out.
The number to carry off: with the size divided out, the depth averages zero on the corner: the corner, v = h in every direction, is the circle a shape's depth is measured from.
