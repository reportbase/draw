Every other page reads a curve from nowhere in particular: N leaves spaced evenly in the curve's own parameter, the whole loop at once, the surveyor's view. This page reads the same curves from somewhere: a reader that stands at a point and holds only what it reads from there.

The situated reader's reading is v/h, how far across against how far along, and it has a corner where the two are equal. From that one idea: where the corner falls says how wide a shape is; a glowing outline seen from far and from near shows two different sizes; there are places a reader standing anywhere cannot see; and when one reader is not enough, the way to add more is to put the next one where the last saw furthest, which is a recursion. Every number on the page is measured as you scroll, with the same arithmetic as the leaf editor's lab drawer, and the gold reader can be dragged.

tvf.situated.js is the situated reader: readings laid along an outline from a standpoint, the four numbers of its opening, the corner read back as a breadth, the glowing outline's two sizes, first meetings, and the rule that puts a child reader where its parent saw furthest, all ported unchanged from the leaf editor's labs.
The number to carry off: the circle read from its centre opens at ½, 1, 2/π, 2/π, and every other shape and standpoint is read as a departure from it.
