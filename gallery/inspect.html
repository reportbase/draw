tvf inspector

The galleries go from a description to a shape. This goes the other way: give it a closed curve and it reads the description back off the leaves. It reports the spectrum, rotational and mirror symmetry, width, and how few leaves would hold it, and links each finding to the gallery that explains it. Send a shape here with inspect the selection in the leaf editor's settings menu, paste .tvf text below, or start from an example.
