tvf epicycles tvf.epicycles.js

A closed curve is a chain of circles, each riding on the rim of the one before, each turning a whole number of times while the pen goes round once. That is Ptolemy's machine for the planets, and it is also, exactly, what this format does between its leaves.

N leaves give a fixed centre and N − 1 circles (one more when N is even: the fastest circle splits into a pair turning opposite ways). The cardinal kernel adds them up leaf by leaf; the chain adds the same sum circle by circle, and the two agree to rounding. So everything the other pages say about harmonics can be watched here as machinery: a symmetric shape is a chain with most of its circles missing, a corner is a chain that never ends, and dropping the small circles is the gentlest way there is to make a shape simpler. tvf.epicycles.js is the discrete Fourier transform of a shape's leaves read as complex numbers, the chain drawn from it, what dropping circles costs, and the way back: any chain, sampled at twice its fastest circle plus one, is a shape this format holds exactly.
