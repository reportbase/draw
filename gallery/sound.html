tvf heard tvf.sound.js

A closed curve read around its parameter is a periodic signal, so one channel of a shape is a single-cycle waveform — which is what a wavetable is. There is no conversion here and no new object: the same numbers that draw the curve make the tone. A circle is a sine. Not an analogy; the x channel of a circle is a cosine, so the tone is a pure tone, and the whole catalogue sits on that.

Click a tile to play it. Which channel you listen to is a choice — x and y are the stored arrays, r is the radius from the centroid, and on a circle r is constant, so a circle's radius is silence. The last section renders both a band-limited and a naive player offline and measures what folds back, which is the one guarantee this format has that you can hear break.

tvf.sound.js is three functions: take a channel, take its harmonics, hand them to createPeriodicWave. The one subtlety is the half-shift — the leaves sit at (k+½)·2π/N, so a plain DFT rotates every harmonic and the mistake is inaudible, because the ear does not hear absolute phase. It shows up only when you draw the waveform and it does not match the shape you drew it from.
