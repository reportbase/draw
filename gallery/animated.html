The loop showed that time is one more circle: keys at even places round it, blended by the kernel that reconstructs a shape, so a key is exact at its own moment and the loop has no seam. The leaf editor's live morph is that idea with controls on it, and these are animations made with it, each one click from the editor: open in draw opens it there playing, with the morph bar at the bottom holding every control, so you can change it, record it as a video or a gif, or keep a pose.

Each tile is a small drawing and what to do with it, written into the link: which shapes go live as a morph, which line they ride, the effect, the trail and the timing (the line under each drawing). Exact shapes blend corner by corner and stay sharp; the detail effects act on a shape's harmonics, so those examples come in as leaves. An example replaces the drawing in the editor's tab, asking first when the tab holds a drawing of yours.

The showpieces come first: each shape a morph of its own, on its own clock (a rate and a phase), its own path (laps and a start), its own spin, colour that blends with its keys, and its own trail and effect, dozens of them at once on one loop that closes. The first steps after them take one idea at a time.
