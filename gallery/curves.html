tvf the menagerie tvf.curves.js

The classical named curves, sorted by the only property this format cares about: their band. Some of them are exactly a tvf — a finite trigonometric sum, machine-zero at sixteen leaves, nothing approximated. Some are analytic and converge faster than any power of 1/N. And some carry a singularity and cost a fixed convergence order forever.

The organizing fact, and it is not the one you would guess: the band belongs to the parameterization, not to the picture. The astroid has four cusps and band 3. A square has four corners and no finite band at all. Bernoulli's lemniscate sits in two different tiers depending on which of its two standard parameterizations you write down — same set of points, verified against the same equation to 6×10⁻¹⁶. Everything below is measured in this tab.

the menagerie three tiers cusps & corners trochoids modular
tvf.curves.js carries eighteen parameterizations, a band measurement at two tolerances (which is what separates a spectrum that terminates from one that merely decays), and the trochoid closure rule. Every curve here is an ordinary Shape once sampled — the .tvf button on each tile copies it.
The finding worth carrying off this page: a corner costs O(1/N) in arc length. Stall the parameterization at the corner with m vanishing derivatives and it costs O(1/Nm+1) — measured 2.00, 4.00, 8.00, 16.00. The corner was never the whole story.
