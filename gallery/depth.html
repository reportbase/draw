tvf depth tvf.depth.js

Packing more leaves into one sweep is breadth. Depth is the same sweep again, entered over one octave of the reading, with its own home, corner and far wall, and so on down. A reader at a point holds a shape as a depth file: one sweep of sixteen values for its own reading, and one more for every octave it enters, each holding only what the levels above it left.

Every shape here is a unit circle with bumps on it, read from a point on its edge, so what is held is the shape's departure from the reader's own circle, and every miss is measured against the shape's formula. The page writes depth files the three ways the format has been written (every octave; only where needed; with every row flat at its children's walls), and every number is measured in your tab, over the whole range from the reader's home to its far wall.

the octaves only where needed the walls depth against breadth
tvf.depth.js is the depth engine and its writers, copied unchanged from the leaf editor (the nested sweep, the theorem's kernel mirrored about home, enter-every-octave, enter-where-needed and flat walls), with the closed-form shapes this page reads.
The number to carry off: a depth file holds a shape to 10⁻⁵ of its departure over the whole range when every sweep leaves what it hands down flat at its children's walls; without that, every file misses by percents in bands beside the walls, however many octaves it enters. And depth beats breadth where the detail is beside the reader, not everywhere.
