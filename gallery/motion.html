tvf the loop tvf.motion.js

The catalogue argues in still frames for a format whose best property is motion. This page is the argument running. Nothing here is a new mechanism: a track is one channel of leaf values, read at a phase, reconstructed by the same kernel that reconstructs a shape from its own. Time is a circle, so a loop has no seam for exactly the reason a closed curve has no seam — there is no loop point, there is a circle and a position on it.

The keyframe tiles are the claim at its sharpest. K shapes sit at the K leaf positions of the time circle and blend with the cardinal kernel, which is 1 at its own node and 0 at the others — so the blend is each key exactly, the weights sum to one at every phase, and there is no easing curve to choose because the band limit is the easing. Evidence at the bottom, measured on load.

tvf.motion.js adds one class and one function. Track is a Float64Array and an at(t) that calls the library's kernel; keyframes() places shapes at leaf positions and blends by the same kernel. There is no timeline object, no easing library, and no wrap handling, because none of those is a thing a circle needs.
Every tile's .tvf button copies the frame you are looking at — the blend at this phase is an ordinary shape, so a moment of an animation pastes into the leaf editor like anything else.
