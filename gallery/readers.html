tvf between readers tvf.between.js

Readers standing apart share no address, no nearness and no unit. What passes between them is a relation among readings of things both read. From relations and turns they can find each other, match their readings place by place, and recover a world up to a scale; the unit is the one thing only an agreement gives.

This is the ladder of Serial, Parallel and Nowhere, §7 and §8, built in the plane: the cross ratio that every reader gets the same; three readers placing each other from shared places and one far reading; a third reader checking which reading is which place, the step the recovered page assumed. Every number is measured in your tab; the readers can be dragged.

tvf.between.js is what passes between readers: each reader's quotient v/h in its own facing, the cross ratio of four, three readers placed from shared turns by one linear solve, and matching readings to places by a third reader's lines.
