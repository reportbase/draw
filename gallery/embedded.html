tvf embedded draw.html?embed=1

The animations again, made to leave the editor. Every tile plays as one SVG file: the scene turned into its picture by the leaf editor, once, and then playing on its own, all of them at once, with no script and no editor behind it. copy svg gives that file, the lightest way to put one on a page: paste it in and it moves.

live plays a tile in the editor itself, the exact way (draw.html?embed=1, the canvas alone), and copy embed code gives that as an <iframe> whose address carries the whole scene. The SVG is the editor's own export: each motion with as many frames as it needs, a shape that only moves and turns written once and moved, trails as the motion begun earlier. Neither way reads or writes the drawing saved in your editor.

A tile's SVG is what the editor's svg button on the morph bar writes; its embed code is an <iframe> of draw.html?embed=1#scene=…, the drawing and the live morph's settings deflated into the address. Make your own in the leaf editor, or start from any tile on the animated page and change the scene there.
