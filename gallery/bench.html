The catalogue shows what the format makes. This page shows why it works, assembled one piece at a time, with every number computed in front of you rather than quoted. It starts with a single function and ends with a closed curve; nothing is added along the way that is not on the screen already.

Read it in order. Each step earns the next one, and step five is the one worth staying for — a mistake that leaves every sample exactly right and everything between them wrong, which is the failure mode the whole design is arranged to avoid.
