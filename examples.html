tvf examples examples

Animations to open in the editor, each one click from here. Every card is a small drawing and what to do with it: which shapes go live as a morph, which line they ride, the effect, the trail and the timing. open in draw opens the editor with the example playing, and the morph bar at the bottom holds every control, so you can change it, record it as a video or a gif, or keep a pose.

An example replaces the drawing in the editor's tab (it asks first when the tab holds a drawing of yours), and exit in the morph bar leaves its shapes as ordinary shapes. The link carries the whole example, so a card's link can be shared as it is.
